# AITradra Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want AITradra to be a respectful, useful and safe open-source community for developers, designers, traders, researchers, students and founders.

We pledge to make participation welcoming regardless of experience level, background, location, language, identity, or financial knowledge.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent, but accept correction quickly.
- Give feedback that helps the project improve.
- Keep discussions focused on product, research, safety and engineering.
- Be honest about uncertainty, limitations and risk.
- Help new contributors understand the codebase.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, intimidation, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discrimination or exclusionary behavior.
- Spam, scams, pump-and-dump behavior, referral spam, or financial solicitation.
- Promising guaranteed profits or encouraging reckless real-money trading.
- Posting private keys, API keys, account credentials, personal financial data, or private user data.
- Pressuring users to trade or invest.

## Financial safety expectation

AITradra is not a place for financial promises. Community members may discuss engineering, data, testing, risk, market analysis, and research methods. They should not present outputs as guaranteed investment advice or guaranteed profitable signals.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close issues, restrict participation, or block users who violate this Code of Conduct.

If you see a problem, open a private/security-safe communication channel with the maintainer or use GitHub's report tools.

## Attribution

This policy is inspired by common open-source community standards and adapted for AITradra's financial-software safety context.
