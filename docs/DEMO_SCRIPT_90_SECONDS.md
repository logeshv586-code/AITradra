# AITradra 90-Second Demo Script

Goal: show AITradra as an explainable market-intelligence product, not a guaranteed-profit trading bot.

## 0-5 seconds — Hook

Narration:

> What if a stock-research app did not just say buy or sell, but showed what happened, why it may have happened, what the agents agree on, and why risk may block the trade?

Visual:

- AITradra home screen
- Global market dashboard
- Left sidebar visible

## 5-15 seconds — Search a ticker

Narration:

> Let us research AAPL.

Visual:

- Enter `AAPL`
- Open Stock Terminal / research view

## 15-30 seconds — Data collection

Narration:

> AITradra collects market data, recent price history, news evidence and available sentiment signals.

Visual:

- Price card
- Daily move
- Evidence/news panel
- Data-source indicators

## 30-45 seconds — Multi-agent analysis

Narration:

> Specialist agents review technicals, macro context, sentiment, catalysts, sector behavior and risk.

Visual:

- Agent cards
- Technical/risk/news summaries
- Consensus area

## 45-60 seconds — Prediction and explanation

Narration:

> The result is not just a signal. AITradra explains the direction, confidence, main driver and what to watch next.

Visual:

- Prediction
- Confidence
- Main reason
- Risk level
- What to watch next

## 60-75 seconds — Risk gate

Narration:

> If confidence and risk do not pass the required gate, AITradra blocks autonomous entry instead of forcing a trade.

Visual:

- Risk Manager block/hold message
- Practice vs real trading separation

## 75-90 seconds — Practice and safe execution

Narration:

> Users can practice with simulated capital. Real orders require explicit confirmation, fresh price data, stop-loss, take-profit and server-side authorization.

Visual:

- Paper trading order
- Fees/slippage shown
- Real trading locked/fail-closed message

Closing:

> AITradra — open-source AI-native market intelligence. Research first. Risk first. Trading only through safety gates.

## Recording checklist

- Use clean browser zoom.
- Do not show API keys or private account information.
- Keep all claims conservative.
- Show risk blocking a trade as a strength.
- End with GitHub URL and star/contribute call-to-action.
