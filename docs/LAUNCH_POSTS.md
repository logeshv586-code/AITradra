## LinkedIn founder post

I have been building **AITradra**, an open-source AI-native market intelligence platform.

The goal is simple: instead of giving users a black-box buy/sell answer, AITradra tries to show:

- what moved
- why it may have moved
- which evidence supports the view
- what multiple AI agents agree or disagree about
- prediction confidence
- risk level
- whether a trade should be blocked

The system combines market data, news, social provenance, technical analysis, multi-agent reasoning, prediction history, paper trading and safety-gated execution.

Important: this is not a guaranteed-profit trading bot. Practice mode is the default, and real-money execution is intentionally fail-closed.

I am looking for:

- contributors
- AI/ML developers
- fintech builders
- quant/trading researchers
- early users
- feedback from investors/design partners

GitHub: https://github.com/logeshv586-code/AITradra

If this space interests you, I would love feedback.

## Reddit / Hacker News style post

Title:

Open-source multi-agent market intelligence app with risk-gated trading

Body:

I am building AITradra, an open-source platform that combines market data, news evidence, social-data provenance, technical analysis, multi-agent AI reasoning, prediction tracking, paper trading and gated broker execution.

The goal is not to promise profitable trades. The goal is to make financial AI more explainable and safer:

- show the evidence behind a view
- compare specialist agent outputs
- track prediction confidence
- label stale/unavailable data honestly
- keep paper trading as default
- block entries that do not pass risk gates
- require explicit confirmation and protective levels for real orders

I am looking for feedback from developers, AI builders, market-data people and fintech users.

GitHub: https://github.com/logeshv586-code/AITradra
