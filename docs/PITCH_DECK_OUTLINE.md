# AITradra Pitch Deck Outline

Use this to create a 10-12 slide investor deck. Keep all financial claims conservative and evidence-based.

## Slide 1 — Title

**AITradra**

Open-source AI-native market intelligence infrastructure.

Subtitle:

**Research → Evidence → Prediction → Risk → Trade**

## Slide 2 — Problem

Market research is fragmented:

- data, charts, news, sentiment and trading live in separate tools
- most AI trading products are black boxes
- retail users rarely understand why a signal was produced
- model confidence is often not connected to risk controls
- live-trading tools can make unsafe behavior too easy

## Slide 3 — Solution

AITradra provides one workflow:

1. collect market data and evidence
2. run specialist AI agents
3. compare agreement and disagreement
4. produce a prediction with confidence
5. explain risk
6. practice trade or route real orders through safety gates

## Slide 4 — Product Demo

Show the user journey:

- enter ticker
- price and news load
- agents analyze
- prediction appears
- risk manager explains decision
- practice trade shows fees/slippage
- live order remains fail-closed unless explicitly enabled
